About

I paint in oils, mostly wildlife, mostly slowly. I'm interested in habitat as much as the animals in it — the space, water, and cover that let something keep living where it is.

Painting sits alongside medicine for me. One is close observation of the body; the other is close observation of everything else. They feed each other more than I expected.
